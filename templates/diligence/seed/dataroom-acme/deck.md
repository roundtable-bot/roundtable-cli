## 1. Company

Acme Ledger automates accounts-payable reconciliation and month-end close for mid-market finance teams (50 to 500 employees). We match invoices, purchase orders and bank lines automatically, route exceptions to the right approver, and close the books days earlier.

- Founded 2022, Denver, Colorado.
- 38 employees.
- Raising a $12.0M Series A to scale sales and product.

## 2. Problem

A typical mid-market controller spends 7 to 8 business days closing each month. Most of that time goes to chasing invoice exceptions across email, spreadsheets and the ERP. Errors found late force restatements and delay audits.

## 3. Product

- **Ledger Match**: three-way matching of invoices, purchase orders and receipts, with a 91% auto-match rate in production.
- **Exception Desk**: routes mismatches to approvers with the evidence attached.
- **Close Board**: a checklist and sign-off tracker for the monthly close.
- Connectors for the five most common mid-market ERPs. Implementation takes four to eight weeks and is billed as a one-time fee.
